Map AI onto the Research Process

A traffic-light activity for responsible, human-in-the-loop scholarship

Purpose

Examine where AI can augment research and where human judgment, verification, and accountability must remain primary. You will first evaluate broad research stages, then reconsider them by examining the specific tasks within each stage.

Traffic-light framework

GREEN — AI-supportive. AI can perform a substantial portion of the task with routine human review.
YELLOW — AI-assisted. AI may contribute, but expert oversight, independent verification, or additional safeguards are essential.
RED — Human-led or restricted. Do not delegate the task: it requires accountable scholarly judgment, or the data, tool, or context make AI use inappropriate.
Color the use, not the technology. The same task may receive different colors depending on the data, tool, stakes, and verification process.
DATA OVERRIDE: Entering identifiable, confidential, proprietary, or otherwise protected data into an unapproved AI system is RED, regardless of the task.

Part 1 — Macro-level mapping

Individually, 4–5 minutes. Rate each research stage on your initial judgment. Use the star for the stage with the greatest potential for useful AI augmentation, and the shield for the stage where human judgment most needs protection.

Part 2 — Task-level mapping

Pairs or groups of three, 7–8 minutes. Rate each task. When you disagree, do not immediately compromise — identify the condition that changes the color.

3 minutes. Return to Part 1 and change any ratings the task-level analysis altered. Record what shifted and why.
